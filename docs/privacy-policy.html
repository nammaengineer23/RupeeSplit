RupeeSplit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RupeeSplit ("RupeeSplit", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the RupeeSplit mobile application and related services (the "Service"). RupeeSplit helps people organise trips, record shared expenses, calculate balances and coordinate settlements.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may receive and process account identifiers and the profile information made available by Google, such as your name, email address and profile image.
  • Trip and expense information: Information you enter or upload, such as trip names, destinations, dates, participant details, expense descriptions, amounts, currencies, payers, split amounts, notes, group-fund records, itinerary details and settlement status.
  • Invitations and sharing: Information needed to create or redeem trip invitation links and QR codes and to associate an invited person with a trip.
  • Contact selection: If you choose a contact-selection feature, the app may access the contact details you select for an invitation. We do not intend to upload or collect your entire address book through that action.
  • Device and diagnostic information: The app and its service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Service, such as app version, device/OS details, authentication and security events, and error diagnostics, where available.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; create and manage trips; record expenses and group-fund activity; calculate balances and settlement suggestions; manage trip membership and invitations; sync information between a user's devices and authorised trip members; provide support; prevent abuse; maintain security; and improve reliability.

3. Where information is processed and stored

RupeeSplit uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support sign-in and store/synchronise app data. Information may be processed on Google's infrastructure in accordance with Google's terms and privacy documentation. Information may also be processed by other service providers where necessary to operate a feature that you choose to use.

Trip information may be visible to other people who are authorised members of that trip. Do not add information to a shared trip unless you are comfortable sharing it with its members.

4. AI-assisted features and third-party services

If you use an AI-assisted feature in the app, the text or other content you submit for that feature may be sent to the relevant AI service provider to generate a response. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable with that processing. The applicable provider's privacy terms also apply to its processing.

The Service may rely on Google sign-in, Firebase and platform services. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide the Service: with members of trips you join, with service providers that operate app functionality, when you direct us to share an invitation or trip information, or when disclosure is required to comply with law, protect users, or address security and abuse.

6. Data security

We use service-level security controls and access restrictions intended to protect account and trip information. Access to trip data is intended to be limited to authenticated users who are authorised members of the relevant trip. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain legitimate business and security records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information using the contact details below. Some information may remain for a limited period in backups or where retention is legally required. Information already shared with other trip members may remain visible to those members where necessary to preserve their trip records.

8. Children's privacy

RupeeSplit is not designed specifically for children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Your choices

You can choose whether to sign in, what trip and expense information to enter, which trips to join, and whether to use optional contact-selection or AI-assisted features. You can stop using the Service at any time and contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Your continued use of the Service after an update means the revised policy applies to subsequent use.
